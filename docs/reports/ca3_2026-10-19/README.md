The report distinguishes the **completed full-fold-0 EchoFusion A/P/D experiment** from the **ongoing paused larger EchoFusion/WallFusion study**. Full-fold does not mean all five folds and seeds are complete. Broad fusion improvement does not establish waveform-specific benefit; zero/swap controls and the retrospective primary-condition uncertainty are reported. No unfinished checkpoint is relabeled as a completed study.

Figures and completed experiment summaries are included for review. Checkpoint weights, datasets, generated sample caches, large study outputs, local environment files and temporary QA files are excluded. Full numerical source artifact hashes remain in `evidence_manifest.json`; those local artifact paths identify provenance and are not downloads.

`report_check.json` records visual and numeric checks of the shipped Word/PDF. `build_report.py` rebuilds the Word document from the local text source and figures with python-docx and Pillow. Re-export through Word or an available DOCX renderer and recheck pagination after edits.

This is an AI-assisted working draft; it is not certified AI-free or zero-similarity. Follow the institution's policy before submission. No submission/upload to the college has been made.
