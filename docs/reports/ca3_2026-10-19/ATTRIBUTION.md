# Report figure attribution

AcousticRooms source: Meta Platforms, Inc. and Liu et al., *Hearing Anywhere in Any Environment*, CVPR 2025. Dataset: https://github.com/facebookresearch/AcousticRooms. Licence: CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.

`partial_scan.png`, `negative_cases.png` and `blender_comparison.png` adapt AcousticRooms geometry through slicing, rasterization, model overlays or rendering. Changes were made by the EchoSpace project. The report captions and references retain the source attribution.

`learning_curves.png` plots project training logs. `pipeline.png` and `architecture.png` are project diagrams. `simulation_live.png` and `observation_live.png` are actual local application screenshots; `simulation_plate.png` arranges those screenshots side by side. They show procedural synthetic capture rather than new measured hardware.

No raw dataset, processed sample, recording or model checkpoint is included. The repository MIT licence applies to project code, not to the underlying AcousticRooms data.
